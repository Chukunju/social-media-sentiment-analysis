## Strengths
- **Performance**: 12 mentions, 58% positive, 8% negative. Commenters praise historical models, strong traction, and low profiles.
- **Price / value**: 23 mentions, 52% positive, 26% negative. Some users feel certain models are worth the hype.

## Weaknesses
*(Note: Sentiment is scored per comment, not per aspect, and the classifier tends to under-detect negative comments, so weaknesses may be understated.)*
- **Comfort / fit**: 54 mentions, 31% positive, 28% negative. Evidence: thin (mentions under 10 for other aspects). Users report narrow sizing, lack of half sizes above 13, and inadequate daily walking support.
- **Price / value**: 23 mentions, 52% positive, 26% negative. Commenters criticize recent price hikes for zoom units and minimal tech relative to cost.

## Suggested upgrades
- **Comfort / fit sizing and ergonomics**: Address fit complaints by expanding half-size availability past size 13 and adjusting the toe box width to support natural foot splay, as commenters report narrow fits and sizing gaps.
- **Price and technology alignment**: Review pricing models to better match the included materials and tech features, mitigating user perceptions of overpricing.

## Confidence and limits
Analysis is limited to 235 comments and 4 tracked aspects. Aspects with fewer than 10 mentions (camera and performance) have thin evidence.
