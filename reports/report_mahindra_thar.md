- **Comfort / fit:** 6 mentions, 50% positive (thin evidence). Reviewers note good driveability for diverse age groups.

### Weaknesses
*Note: Weaknesses may be understated as our classifier tends to under-detect negative comments.*
- **Price / value:** 15 mentions, 33% negative (net -7). Commenters debate local affordability, with some calling Australian pricing insane ($15k–$30k AUD) while others praise its capability and value compared to global options.
- **Camera:** 9 mentions, 44% negative (net -11) (thin evidence). Critics report heavy shaking during suspension tests, while positive remarks praise video production and editing work.

### Suggested upgrades
- **Camera stabilization:** Based on user complaints of dashboard-mounted camera shaking during drives, the product team might hypothesize that improving optical or physical stabilization will enhance video review quality.
- **Roof and exterior updates:** Based on feedback concerning plastic roof heat, color fading, and outdated grilles, the team might hypothesize that transitioning to a metal roof and integrating modern LED features will improve user satisfaction.

### Confidence and limits
Analysis is limited to 183 comments with low overall mention volumes per aspect (max 15). Four aspects contained thin evidence (fewer than 10 mentions), restricting the generalizability of these findings.
